# Net 30 terms

An invoice is payable within 30 days of its date. [[entities/damp-harbor-hotel]] pays on these terms ([[sources/2026-09-14-damp-harbor-hotel-agreement]]), so the due date of any invoice is the invoice date plus 30 days.
