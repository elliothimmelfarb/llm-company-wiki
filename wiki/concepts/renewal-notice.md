# Renewal notice

A written statement to a landlord, given by a fixed date, that a tenant wants to stay. Under the lease with [[entities/puddlemere-property-trust]] it is due at least 90 days before 2028-02-29, which is 2027-12-01 [inferred from clause 3] ([[sources/2026-03-01-workshop-lease]]). Missing it ends the lease.
