# Workshop lease, 2026-03-01

**Why it matters:** the shop loses its premises on 2028-02-29 unless written notice is given 90 days before.

## Key facts

- [[entities/puddlemere-property-trust]] lets 4 Gutter Row to [[entities/bumblewick-umbrella-repair]] (preamble).
- The term runs from 2026-03-01 to 2028-02-29 (clause 1).
- Rent is $900, due on the first of each month (clause 2).
- Renewal needs written notice at least 90 days before the term ends (clause 3). See [[concepts/renewal-notice]].
- The premises may only be used for repairing umbrellas and similar articles (clause 4).
