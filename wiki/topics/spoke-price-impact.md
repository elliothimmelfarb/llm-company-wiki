# Spoke price impact

The spoke rises by $0.25 on 2026-11-01 ([[sources/2026-10-02-canopy-spoke-price-email]]). An average hotel repair uses 3 spokes ([[sources/2026-10-20-owners-meeting]]), so each repair costs $0.75 more, and a full batch of 40 costs $30 more [inferred].

[[entities/marigold-bumblewick]] decided to ask [[entities/damp-harbor-hotel]] for $20 per umbrella from the third batch. The draft letter is in `outputs/2026-10-20-hotel-rate-letter-draft.md`.
